Platform overview

Customer organisationsExit Brain team

Two separate worlds. Customer organisations work inside their own workspace, isolated by row-level security. The Exit Brain team uses a separate console that sees account metadata and system health, never evidence, answers or handovers, unless a customer owner grants time-limited support access.

Manager runs a transition

ManagerOwner / Admin

The core loop: read the work records, confirm what matters, interview, verify, and approve a handover the successor can trust.

flowchart TD
  A[Dashboard] --> B[Start a transition
person, GitHub login, last day, successor, repos]
  B --> B1{Already an active transition
for this person?}
  B1 -->|Yes| B2[Open the existing one]
  B1 -->|No| C[Reading GitHub
per-repo progress, pauses for rate limits, retry]
  C --> D[Knowledge areas detected
each with evidence, risk bars, confidence]
  D --> E{Manager review}:::gate
  E -->|Confirm| F[Confirmed area]
  E -->|Edit| F
  E -->|Reject| X[Rejected, kept in history]
  F --> G[Interview questions generated
3 to 6 per area, each cites evidence]
  G --> H[[Leaving employee answers and approves]]
  H --> I[Answers verified against records
supported, partly, conflicting, unverified]
  I --> I1{Conflicting?}
  I1 -->|Yes| I2[Manager task: resolve or override with comment]
  I1 -->|No| J
  I2 --> J[Handover generated
every statement cited with status]
  J --> K{Approve handover}:::gate
  K -->|Changes needed| J
  K -->|Approve| L[Locked version shared with successor]
  L --> M[Track successor readiness]
  classDef gate stroke:#9a5d00,stroke-width:2px
Gate 1: review areasOnly confirmed or edited areas become interview questions.
Gate 3: approve handoverDialog lists open items and exactly who gains access before approving.
Risk is not performanceRisk describes knowledge concentration in code areas, never a person’s quality of work.

Leaving employee

Leaving employee

Short sessions, evidence for every question, and nothing shared until they approve it.

flowchart TD
  A[Invitation email] --> B[Accept invite
must match the confirmed email]
  B --> C[My interview
why this exists, what is shared, about 15 min a day]
  C --> D[Pick a topic, highest risk first]
  D --> E[Question with evidence
Why are we asking?]
  E --> F{Answer}
  F -->|Write answer| G[Assessed: complete, vague, incomplete]
  F -->|I don't know / someone else| S[Skip with reason or suggest a person]
  G -->|Vague or incomplete| FU[Follow-up question
max 2 per question]
  FU --> F
  G -->|Complete| N[Draft saved, only visible to them]
  S --> N
  N --> P[Preview what will be shared]
  P --> Q{Approve answers}:::gate
  Q -->|Edit first| E
  Q -->|Approve| R[Shared with manager and successor
locked, cannot be edited]
  R -.->|successor escalates a question| E
  classDef gate stroke:#9a5d00,stroke-width:2px
Gate 2: employee approvalDrafts are invisible to everyone else (RLS); approval is explicit.
Never an evaluationNo scoring of the person, only of whether an answer is specific enough to hand over.

Successor

Successor

Waits for an approved handover, then learns in risk order and asks questions answered only from approved knowledge.

flowchart TD
  A[Accept invite] --> B{Handover approved?}
  B -->|Not yet| W[Waiting state
explains what will arrive and when]
  W -.->|manager approves| B
  B -->|Yes| C[Learning checklist
high risk first, readiness meter]
  C --> D[Open checklist item]
  D --> E[Handover section with citations]
  E --> F[Evidence panel
commit, PR or issue excerpt]
  C --> G[Ask the assistant]
  G --> H{Found in approved knowledge?}
  H -->|Yes| I[Answer with sources]
  H -->|No| J[Says it can’t find it]
  J --> K[Ask the leaving employee or manager]
  K --> L[Question added to their interview]
  L -.->|answered and approved| I
  D --> M[Mark done]
  M --> N[Readiness updates for manager and HR]

System states

Platform admin console

Exit Brain teamNew: not in PRD yet

A separate internal console for running the business and the service. Staff sign in with SSO and MFA from an allowlist; they are not members of any customer organisation.

flowchart TD
  A[Staff sign-in
SSO + MFA, staff allowlist] --> B[Console home
active pilots, open incidents, job failures]
  B --> C[Organisations list
name, plan, seats, transitions, status]
  C --> D[Organisation detail
metadata only: plan, usage, integration health]
  D --> D1[Usage and billing
departures used, $149 per departure pilot]
  D --> D2[Integration health
GitHub installation status, failed jobs, rate-limit pauses]
  D --> D3{Need to see content?}
  D3 -->|No| D4[Resolve from metadata and logs]
  D3 -->|Yes| SA[Request support access from the customer owner]
  B --> E[Platform health
job queue, AI run errors, token spend vs $5 target]
  B --> F[Plans and feature flags]
  B --> G[Deletion requests
confirm retention and erasure jobs ran]
  B --> H[Staff audit log
every console action recorded]
No content by defaultEvidence, answers and handovers are unreadable from the console. Enforced in the database, not the UI.
Everything auditedStaff actions go to a staff audit log; anything touching a tenant is also visible in that tenant’s audit log.
Billing is post-MVPUsage counts can show in the MVP; invoicing and payments come later (PRD non-goal).

Support access

Support engineerCustomer ownerNew: not in PRD yet

How the Exit Brain team helps with a specific problem without standing access to customer knowledge.

sequenceDiagram
  autonumber
  participant C as Customer owner
  participant EB as Exit Brain app
  participant S as Support engineer
  C->>EB: Report a problem with a transition
  EB->>S: Ticket with org and transition id, no content
  S->>EB: Request access, scope and reason
  EB->>C: Approval request: scope, reason, 24 hours
  C->>EB: Approve, or decline
  EB->>S: Read-only access to that transition, expires automatically
  S->>EB: Investigate and fix
  EB->>C: Every view and action appears in the customer audit log
  Note over EB: Access ends at expiry or when the owner revokes it
